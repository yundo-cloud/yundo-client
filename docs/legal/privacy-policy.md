# Yundo Privacy Policy

Last updated: `2026-08-15`

Effective date: `2026-08-15`

This policy applies to the Yundo client and its associated account, device, connection, rules, traffic, and issue-reporting services. Yundo does not sell personal information.

Yundo is the service provider and data controller for these services. For privacy, data-rights, or security questions, contact `privacy@yundo.app`. For general product and account support, contact `support@yundo.app`.

## 1. Our principles

Yundo processes only the information necessary to provide the service, protect security, and investigate failures. Yundo does not build browsing profiles, use personal information for advertising, or sell or rent personal information.

Network forwarding technically requires real-time processing of destination addresses and connection data. Yundo's backend does not retain users' website history, complete URLs, search content, webpage content, chat content, file content, or DNS query details.

## 2. Information we process

### 2.1 Accounts and sign-in

- Email address, password hash, account status, sign-in state, and session tokens. The email address identifies the account and is used for sign-in and necessary account, security, and service notices. The service does not store passwords in plaintext.
- The time, result, IP address, User-Agent, and necessary error codes for registration, sign-in, and security checks, used for rate limiting, abuse prevention, and security auditing.
- Optional contact information that a user voluntarily provides in an issue report. Yundo does not require contact information beyond what is needed for the account.

### 2.2 Devices and versions

- An anonymous device identifier generated by the app or derived from secure system storage, device name, platform, operating-system version, app version, sign-in time, and last-active time.
- Yundo does not read or upload hardware serial numbers and does not upload the raw Windows `MachineGuid`.

### 2.3 Plans, activation, and traffic

- Activation-code redemption results, plan, validity period, device limit, monthly traffic quota, and period-entitlement snapshots.
- Acceleration-session start and end times, status, selected region, device, uploaded/downloaded bytes, and period traffic ledger.
- Necessary traffic summaries from the node side, used to display usage, enforce quotas, and investigate measurement anomalies.

### 2.4 User-saved access preferences

Domains and access preferences that users actively add are stored in the account and synchronized to other devices signed in to the same account. They are configurations explicitly created by the user, not browsing history collected from actual traffic. Users can view, edit, or delete them in the app.

### 2.5 Local records and diagnostics

- Records shown in the app are temporarily stored on the current device for the user's own review. Turning records off stops new entries; signing out or signing in again clears these temporary records.
- Active network diagnostics and the latest acceleration-process record are stored on the current device by default and are not uploaded merely because the diagnostics page is opened.
- When a user submits an issue report, Yundo receives the description, optional contact information, and allowlisted, redacted information such as app version, platform, operating-system version, rule version, stable error codes, and failure stage.
- Issue reports do not include screenshots, passwords, activation codes, complete device UUIDs, complete node keys, or connection private keys.

Yundo does not currently use a third-party crash analytics service. If this changes, Yundo will update this policy and the relevant in-app controls before enabling such a service.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

- Register accounts, sign users in, maintain sessions, send necessary account and service notices, and protect account security.
- Redeem activation codes, provide plan entitlements, enforce device limits, and enforce traffic quotas.
- Generate short-lived connection plans, display status and usage, and start or stop acceleration.
- Synchronize access preferences created by the user.
- Prevent abuse, investigate failures, process user-submitted issues, and maintain service stability.
- Comply with applicable law and protect the lawful rights and interests of users, Yundo, and third parties.

## 4. Sharing and third-party services

Yundo does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the following services only to the extent necessary to provide the service:

- Yundo-controlled APIs, databases, connection control systems, and node infrastructure for account, connection, rules, traffic, and security functions.
- Apple App Store and TestFlight for installation, updates, test distribution, and Apple's own crash or testing feedback capabilities. Apple processes related information under its privacy policy.
- GitHub when a user actively opens source-code, license, terms-of-service, or privacy-policy links. GitHub processes ordinary access logs under its policies.
- Hosting, network, domain, email, and backup providers required to operate Yundo. These providers should process information only as needed to provide the relevant infrastructure.

Except with authorization, when necessary to provide the service, or when required by law, Yundo does not disclose personal information to other third parties.

## 5. Retention and deletion

- Connection sessions, traffic details, period traffic ledgers, account entitlements, and important security-audit records are retained only for as long as needed to provide the service, keep accounting consistent, prevent abuse, investigate failures, and meet legal obligations.
- Yundo determines specific retention periods based on data type, purpose, risk, and applicable law. When the purpose ends, Yundo deletes or anonymizes the information unless retention is required by law or necessary to resolve a dispute.
- User-created access preferences remain until the user deletes them or the account data is deleted.
- Issue reports remain while the issue is being handled and for the period needed for follow-up troubleshooting. Users may request deletion of identifiable information in a report.
- Local temporary records are retained as described in the app and can be deleted by signing out, signing in again, or clearing records.
- Backup data is overwritten according to the backup-rotation period, except for records that must be retained by law or to handle a security dispute.

Users may submit requests to access, correct, or delete account data at `privacy@yundo.app`. Yundo may verify identity before processing a request and will handle it within a reasonable period. Data that must be retained by law or is necessary to resolve a security dispute may not be deleted immediately.

## 6. Security

Yundo uses HTTPS, password hashing, short-lived access tokens, long-lived refresh tokens, device-session revocation, least privilege, basic rate limiting, field allowlists, and redacted diagnostics to reduce risk. No system can guarantee absolute security. Do not enter passwords, activation codes, private keys, or other secrets in access preferences, optional contact fields, or issue descriptions.

## 7. Your choices

Users can:

- Decline to provide optional contact information or submit an issue report.
- Turn off local records to stop new local entries.
- Delete access preferences they created and devices linked to their account.
- Stop acceleration, sign out, and uninstall the app.
- Contact `privacy@yundo.app` to request access to, correction of, or deletion of account data.

If a user does not agree to the necessary processing described in this policy, account, device, plan, rules-synchronization, and traffic-quota features may no longer be available.

## 8. Children

Yundo is not designed for children. Children should use the service only with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian. If information from a child was processed without appropriate consent, contact us so that we can address it.

## 9. Changes and contact

Yundo will update this policy when the scope of processing, third-party services, or user rights materially changes and will update the date at the top of the page. Important changes may be highlighted so that users can review them.

For privacy questions, data-rights requests, or security reports, contact `privacy@yundo.app`.
